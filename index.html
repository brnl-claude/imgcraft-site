A lightweight Android frontend for Imgur.

ImgCraft is an unofficial app for browsing Imgur on Android, built from scratch on Imgur's v3 API. It shows the feed, posts, comments and profiles, keeps your favourites on your phone, and contains no ads or analytics.

  • Imgur v3 API
  • No ads or analytics
  • Local favourites
  • Video frames and pictures to crop, save or share
  • Browse without an account

Features

ImgCraft is a frontend: the posts, comments and profiles come from Imgur, and the app shows them.

Video frame by frame

Pause a video and tap its left or right side to step one frame back or forward, as quickly as you tap. The camera button takes the frame on screen at the video's own resolution: crop it, save it to your phone, share it, or put it in a comment as a reaction image. Pictures have a crop button that does the same.

Feed and search

Most viral, User sub and Top in a staggered grid with infinite scroll. Search with saved searches, tag pages, and swipe between tabs.

Video playback

Three players are kept ready for the previous, current and next video, and the start of the next videos is loaded ahead, so moving between them is quick.

Comments

Read threads sorted by best, top or newest; deep threads open in place. Once logged in, vote, write and reply, with reaction GIFs.

Favourites in folders

Keep posts, single images or reaction GIFs in folders. They're stored on your phone, not on your Imgur account.

Follow without an account

Follow users and tags on the device and see their new posts under Notifications. A phone notification is there if you turn it on.

Save and share

Save or share one image, the whole album, or a link to the post. Hide tags and users you don't want to see, and dim posts you've seen.

Your library stays on your phone

Favourites, likes and the other lists ImgCraft keeps are stored locally on the device. They aren't sent to Imgur and don't change anything on your Imgur account.

  • Favourites and their folders are local only.
  • Likes is a local list of the posts you upvoted in ImgCraft, kept only when you turn it on. The upvote itself is a normal vote on Imgur.
  • Follows, hidden users and hidden tags are local too, so they work without an account.

Import from Imgur

Once logged in, you can copy your Imgur favourites, favourite folders, followed users and tags, blocked users and muted tags into ImgCraft, each on its own (Settings > Import from Imgur). An import only adds to what's on your phone and changes nothing on Imgur.

Private by default

ImgCraft connects to Imgur's API to show you posts, and to Giphy only if you add your own Giphy key. Anything that remembers what you do is off until you turn it on, and turning it off again deletes it.

What's in the app

  • Two permissions: internet and network state
  • Settings and login stay in app-private storage, left out of backups
  • History, likes, seen posts and recent searches are opt-in
  • Data saving on mobile data or battery saver: videos load on tap
  • Light, dark or pure black theme

What's not

  • No ads or ad SDKs
  • No analytics, crash reporting or Firebase
  • No Google Play services
  • No device ids sent anywhere
  • No account needed to browse

Download

Every version is signed with the same key, so a new one installs over the old one.

  1. Download ImgCraft.apk on your phone and open it to install.
  2. Open ImgCraft and go to Settings.
  3. Paste the client ID of an Imgur API application. ImgCraft doesn't come with one.
  4. Optionally log in to vote, comment and import your favourites.

Open Imgur links in ImgCraft

Share an Imgur link to ImgCraft, or turn on Open supported links for ImgCraft in Android's app settings to open them directly. What changed in each version is under Settings > About.
